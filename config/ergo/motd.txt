Welcome to Omen Private IRC.

This is a small, account-private community network. Public access is available
only through the managed TLS endpoints; every IRC connection still requires a
personal SASL account. Do not share account or operator passwords. Start in
#general; infrastructure notices are in #ops.

History is retained for 30 days on registered channels. Contact the network
operator if you need an invitation or account recovery. Current clients should
use am4.tail8e749c.ts.net:8443 with TLS certificate verification enabled.
