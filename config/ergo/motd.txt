Welcome to Omen Private IRC.

This is a small private community network reachable only through the tailnet.
Authenticate with your personal Ergo account using SASL. Do not share account
or operator passwords. Start in #general; infrastructure notices are in #ops.

History is retained for 30 days on registered channels. Contact the network
operator if you need an account, access recovery, or help trusting the TLS
certificate.
